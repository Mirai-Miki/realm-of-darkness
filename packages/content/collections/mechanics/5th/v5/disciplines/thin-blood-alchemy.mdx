# Thin-Blood Alchemy

**Nicknames:** Cooking, Home Brew, The Craft, Mashup

## Description

ciThin-Blood Alchemy is the youngest of the Disciplines, born from the necessity of the Duskborn. Because Thin-bloods cannot naturally hold the power of the ancients, they have learned to "cook" it. By mixing their own weak vitae with human blood of intense emotional resonance (and often mundane chemicals or narcotics), they can synthesize powers that mimic the Disciplines of true Kindred or create entirely new effects.

This "Discipline" is highly versatile but requires preparation. Alchemists might carry vials of home-brewed power, use their own bodies as living distilieries, or channel the emotional states of their victims directly. It is a scrap-magic of the modern nights, looked down upon by the Camarilla as a cheap imitation, but feared for its unpredictability.

It is exclusive to the **Thin-Blooded** (Duskborn).

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Sorcery
- **Masquerade Threat:** Variable.
  - The threat depends entirely on the power being counterfeited. Brewing the potions itself can look like illegal drug manufacturing, which carries its own mortal risks, but the activation of the powers ranges from invisible (mimicking Auspex) to flashy (mimicking Blood Sorcery).
- **Blood Resonance:** Variable.
  - Alchemy relies entirely on Resonance. To create a potion that mimics a specific Discipline, the Alchemist usually requires blood with the Resonance associated with that Discipline (e.g., Sanguine blood for Presence effects, Choleric for Potence).
