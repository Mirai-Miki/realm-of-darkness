# Celerity

**Nicknames:** Bolting, Slipping, Velocitas, The Dash

## Description

Celerity is the terrifying speed of the predator. It allows a vampire to move faster than the human eye can track, dodge bullets with ease, and strike an enemy before they even realize they are under attack. While Potence is the power to crush, Celerity is the power to be everywhere at once.

For the observer, a vampire using Celerity often appears as a blur or seems to teleport from one location to another. It is a staple Discipline for assassins and warriors, but it is equally prized by messengers and those who simply wish to escape a bad situation before the first punch is thrown. In combat, it offers a supreme advantage: the ability to act while your opponent is still deciding what to do.

It is a core Discipline for the **Banu Haqim**, **Brujah**, and **Toreador**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Physical
- **Masquerade Threat:** Medium to High.
  - Moving slightly faster than a human is easily dismissed as adrenaline or training. However, vanishing from sight, catching a projectile, or moving across a room in a blink are clear breaches of the Masquerade. Modern cameras often capture these feats only as distorted glitches.
- **Blood Resonance:** Choleric.
  - This Discipline responds best to blood rich in stress hormones and adrenaline. The blood of athletes, runners, amphetamine users, and those in a state of terror or "fight or flight" panic provides the strongest fuel.
