# Presence

**Nicknames:** Superstardom, Enthrallment, Sublimitas

## Description

Presence is the power to control the emotions of others. Unlike Dominate, which issues direct mental commands, Presence works on the heart and the gut. It can make a victim fall hopelessly in love with the vampire, freeze in abject terror, or follow them with religious devotion. It is the Discipline of the cult leader, the seducer, and the tyrant.

This power affects crowds as easily as individuals, making it a terrifying tool for social control. A master of Presence can walk into a room and instantly command the attention of everyone present, turning hostility into adoration or fear. However, it is a subtle trap; those affected often believe their feelings are genuine, making them willing pawns who serve out of "love" rather than compulsion.

It is a signature Discipline of the **Toreador**, **Ventrue**, and **Brujah**, as well as the **Ministry** (Setites).

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Mental
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low to Medium.
  - Presence is often invisible. People rarely realize they are being manipulated magically; they simply feel an overwhelming attraction or fear. The risk comes when the effects are too sudden or extreme to be natural, or when a mob forms around the vampire in a way that draws police attention.
- **Blood Resonance:** Sanguine.
  - The Discipline resonates with the blood of the passionate and the adored. The blood of models, movie stars, influencers, politicians, and those who are either deeply in love or lust is ideal fuel.
