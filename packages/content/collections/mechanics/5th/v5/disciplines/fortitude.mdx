# Fortitude

**Nicknames:** Bricking Up, Stone Flesh, Resistentia

## Description

Fortitude is the supernatural toughness that allows a vampire to endure damage that would pulverize a mortal or destroy a lesser Kindred. It is more than just thick skin; it is a mental and physical barrier against the world. While Potence is the power to strike, Fortitude is the power to survive.

Vampires with this Discipline can shrug off bullet wounds, walk through fire, and even resist the burning touch of sunlight for brief periods. In modern nights, it is as much a mental shield as a physical one, allowing practitioners to resist supernatural coercion and remain stoic in the face of political or social pressure. It is the defining tool of bodyguards, warlords, and those who plan to outlast their enemies rather than outrun them.

It is a core Discipline for the **Gangrel**, **Ventrue**, and **Hecata**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Physical
- **Masquerade Threat:** Medium.
  - Taking a punch without flinching is one thing; walking away from a high-speed car crash or ignoring a shotgun blast to the chest is another. Such feats are technically breaches, though they are often rationalized by witnesses as "PCP," "dumb luck," or "special effects" rather than supernatural power.
- **Blood Resonance:** Melancholic.
  - The Discipline feeds on the blood of survivors and those with high endurance. The blood of war veterans, abuse survivors, marathon runners, mountain climbers, and those with exceptionally strong immune systems provides the best fuel.
