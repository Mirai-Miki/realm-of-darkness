# Auspex

**Nicknames:** Voyeurism, Scrying, Anima Visus

## Description

Auspex grants the vampire supernatural senses, allowing them to perceive reality on a level far beyond human capability. It is the power of truth and revelation, enabling a Kindred to spot lies, uncover hidden enemies, and even peer into the future. For many, this Discipline is a double-edged sword: while it offers the ultimate advantage in information gathering, it can also lead to paranoia, as the user becomes acutely aware of every hidden threat and emotional shift around them.

Practitioners of Auspex often serve as spies, detectives, or court advisors. They can read the psychic residue on an object, sense the emotional state of a mortal, or telepathically intrude upon a rival's mind. However, the clarity it provides is addictive; once a vampire tastes the truth that Auspex offers, they often find it difficult to return to the ignorance of mundane senses.

It is a core Discipline for the **Malkavians**, **Toreador**, **Tremere**, and **Hecata** (specifically the Harbingers of Ashur), though it is also common among the **Banu Haqim** and **Ravnos**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Mental
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low.
  - Auspex rarely manifests visible effects. A vampire staring intently or predicting an event can usually be dismissed as having keen intuition or dumb luck. However, entering a trance in public or reacting to unseen stimuli can still raise suspicion.
- **Blood Resonance:** Phlegmatic.
  - The Discipline resonates with the blood of visionaries, artists (particularly photographers), detectives, and those who experience altered states of reality, such as schizophrenics or users of psychoactive substances.
