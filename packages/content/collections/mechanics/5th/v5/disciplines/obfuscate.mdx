# Obfuscate

**Nicknames:** Stealth Mode, Cloaking, Occulto, Veiling

## Description

Obfuscate is the uncanny ability to remain unseen. Unlike true invisibility, which bends light, Obfuscate works primarily on the mind of the observer, forcing them to ignore the vampire's presence. A master of this Discipline can stand in the middle of a crowded room, and people will simply subconsciously choose not to look at them or acknowledge they are there.

This power covers all five senses; victims will not hear the vampire's footsteps or smell their scent while the power is active. However, because it relies on mental manipulation, it has significant limitations: it often fails against electronic surveillance like cameras (unless specific powers are used) and can be broken if the vampire draws attention to themselves by attacking or making loud noises. It is the ultimate tool for spies, assassins, and those who prefer to strike from the shadows.

It is a signature Discipline of the **Malkavians**, **Nosferatu**, **Banu Haqim**, and **Ravnos**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Mental
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low.
  - If the power works as intended, no one sees anything to report. The only risk comes from sudden failures or electronic recordings that catch what the human eye missed.
- **Blood Resonance:** Melancholic.
  - This Discipline resonates with the blood of the socially invisible. The blood of the homeless, the forgotten, spies, pickpockets, backstage roadies, and those who live their lives ignored by the masses is ideal.
