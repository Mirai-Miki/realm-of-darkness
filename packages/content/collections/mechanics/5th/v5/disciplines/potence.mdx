# Potence

**Nicknames:** Hulking, Blood’s Might, Percutio

## Description

Potence is the raw, physical expression of the Beast. While many vampires are naturally stronger than mortals, Potence is the power to crush stone, bend steel, and tear enemies limb from limb. It is not merely a passive enhancement of muscles but a surge of vitae-fueled power that allows a vampire to perform feats of strength that defy physics.

For the **Brujah** and **Nosferatu**, this Discipline is a primary weapon. It allows them to end fights instantly, leap across city streets, or simply intimidate rivals with a display of terrifying power. Unlike Celerity (speed) or Fortitude (toughness), Potence is purely offensive; it is the ability to force the world to break before you do.

It is a core Discipline for the **Brujah**, **Nosferatu**, and **Lasombra**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Physical
- **Masquerade Threat:** Medium to High.
  - A vampire lifting a heavy object might be explained away as "hysterical strength" in a crisis. However, punching through a concrete wall, throwing a motorcycle, or leaping up the side of a building is a blatant breach of the Masquerade that is almost impossible to explain to witnesses.
- **Blood Resonance:** Choleric.
  - This Discipline feeds on the blood of the strong and the aggressive. The blood of bodybuilders, MMA fighters, lumberjacks, dockworkers, and those brimming with rage or testosterone provides the most potent fuel.
