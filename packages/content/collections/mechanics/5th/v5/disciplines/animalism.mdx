# Animalism

**Nicknames:** Doolittling, Taming, Bestiae Sermo

## Description

Animalism bridges the gap between the vampire and the natural world. Where most animals instinctively recoil from the undead, sensing the predator within, a practitioner of Animalism can soothe, communicate with, and command them. This Discipline is not merely about training beasts but establishing a supernatural communion with them.

Kindred who master this art often find it offers a unique respite from the political machinations of the undead; animals act as loyal companions (or _famuli_) who serve without judgment. In practical terms, it allows a vampire to use the eyes of a hawk to scout, a swarm of rats to strip a haven, or a pack of dogs to defend their territory.

Beyond the physical realm, Animalism also grants insight into the metaphysical "Beast" that drives all vampires. Masters of this path can sense the rage in others, quell the frenzy of a coterie mate, or even draw out the feral instincts of a mortal.

It is a signature ability of the **Gangrel** and **Nosferatu**, who often exist on the fringes of mortal society, as well as the **Ravnos** and **Tzimisce**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Mental
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low to Medium.
  - Quietly communicating with an animal often passes as mere eccentricity or skilled handling. However, supernatural displays—such as commanding swarms, possessing an animal's body, or inciting unnatural aggression—can easily draw unwanted attention.
- **Blood Resonance:** Animal Blood.
  - This Discipline is most potently fueled by the blood of animals, specifically feral or predatory creatures that resonate with the wild.
