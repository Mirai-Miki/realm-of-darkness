# Blood Sorcery

**Nicknames:** A Kind of Magic, Thaumaturgy, Quietus

## Description

Blood Sorcery is the practice of manipulating the vitae itself to create magical effects. Unlike other Disciplines that might develop intuitively, Blood Sorcery is a rigorous art that almost always requires a teacher or intense study of occult texts. The **Tremere** refer to it as "Thaumaturgy" and jealously guard its secrets, while the **Banu Haqim** have long practiced a variation known as "Quietus."

This Discipline allows a vampire to turn their blood into a weapon or a tool. It can curdle the blood in a victim's veins, transmute vitae into corrosive acid, or synthesize poisons that affect even the undead. Beyond these direct applications, Blood Sorcery opens the door to **Rituals**—complex magical workings that take time to cast but offer powerful and versatile effects, from warding a haven to communicating over vast distances.

It is a defining Discipline for the **Tremere** and **Banu Haqim**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Sorcery
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low to High.
  - The threat level varies wildly depending on the power used. A subtle ritual might pass unnoticed, while boiling a victim's blood or spitting acidic vitae is an overt display of supernatural power that instantly breaches the Masquerade.
- **Blood Resonance:** Sanguine.
  - While the magic comes from the user's will and knowledge, it responds best to blood rich in occult resonance. The blood of cult leaders, hemophiles, bibliophiles, and human sorcerers is particularly potent fuel.
