# Dominate

**Nicknames:** Snake Charming, Mesmerism, Mentis Imperium

## Description

Dominate is the art of crushing another's will. Through unbroken eye contact and spoken commands, a vampire can force a victim to perform actions against their judgment, erase incriminating memories, or implant complex hypnotic triggers. It is the ultimate tool of control, allowing the Kindred to turn enemies into puppets and maintain the Masquerade by silencing witnesses.

This Discipline typically requires the user to capture the victim's gaze and speak a language the victim understands. It is subtle but brutal; the victim often remembers the experience as a fog or a sudden, inexplicable compulsion to obey. Masters of Dominate can control entire crowds or rewrite a mortal's entire personality over time.

It is a signature Discipline of the **Ventrue**, **Tremere**, **Lasombra**, and some **Tzimisce**.

## Characteristics

- **Type:** Mental
- **Masquerade Threat:** Low.
  - Dominate is invisible to the naked eye. An observer sees only a conversation, and unless the command forces the victim to do something blatantly supernatural or suicidal, it rarely raises suspicion.
- **Blood Resonance:** Phlegmatic.
  - The Discipline feeds on the blood of those accustomed to command or obedience. The blood of CEOs, drill sergeants, cult leaders, submissives, and those with a "control freak" personality (or those who have completely given up control) is ideal.
