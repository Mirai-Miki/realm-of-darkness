# Clan The Ministry

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Tempters, Serpents, Clan of Faith, Clan of Lies, Setites

---

## Overview

The Ministry—historically known as the Followers of Set—are spiritual provocateurs, merchants of liberation, and master manipulators who operate at the dangerous intersection of faith, temptation, and vice. To the Ministry, moral dogmas, legal restrictions, and social conventions are artificial shackles forged by hypocritical elders and tyrannical gods to keep souls enslaved. The Serpents view their sacred calling as one of emancipation: by catering to a victim's hidden cravings, addictions, and forbidden vices, they shatter these internal chains, forcing the subject to confront their authentic self.

Whether running luxury nightclubs, occult street ministries, underground drug distribution rings, or alternative spiritual communes, Ministers always have exactly what their marks desire. They freely dispense drugs, wealth, sensual indulgence, and religious absolution, knowing that every favor owed weaves a tighter web of dependency. However, their devotion to darkness and decay has left their Blood acutely vulnerable to radiance: sunlight blisters their flesh with terrifying speed, and even harsh artificial illumination repels them.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

After being pointedly snubbed during negotiations to join the Camarilla (culminating in the dramatic bombing of a Paris summit), the Ministry rebranded itself and pledged allegiance to the Anarch Movement. Portraying themselves as anti-authoritarian spiritual guides and suppliers of illicit goods, they have become indispensable to Anarch baronies, running free-trade markets and smuggling networks while quietly cultivating religious cults across the globe.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

Ministers recruit mortals possessing magnetic charisma, spiritual conviction, or mastery over human desire. Ideal fledglings include unconventional preachers, nightclub promoters, charismatic con artists, substance-dependency counselors, burlesque performers, and black-market fixers who understand that every human being has a price.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Faith Healer**: A charismatic street preacher who operates a storefront chapel, offering miracles and emotional redemption to desperate mortals while feeding on their devotion.
- **The Luxury Concierge**: A nightclub impresario who caters to high-profile Kindred and mortals, supplying bespoke narcotics, exotic blood vessels, and discrete havens.
- **The Spiritual Demagogue**: An ideological revolutionary who preaches against the false morality of the Camarilla, converting young Anarchs into devoted acolytes.
- **The Black-Market Broker**: A seasoned smuggler who moves stolen relics, counterfeit identities, and mystical paraphernalia through port cities without raising suspicion.
