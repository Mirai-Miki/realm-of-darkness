# Clan Ravnos

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Nomads, Rogues, Ravens, Daredevils, The Haunted

---

## Overview

The Ravnos are an enigmatic, resilient breed of wanderers, tricksters, and risk-takers who have clawed their way back from the brink of total annihilation. Historically misunderstood and persecuted as vagabonds and thieves, the clan suffered an apocalyptic catastrophe during the Week of Nightmares, when their ancient progenitor awoke and was destroyed in a cataclysm of solar bombardment and spiritual fire. The psychic shockwave drove elder Ravnos into cannibalistic frenzy and decimated their numbers across the globe.

Today, the surviving Rogues and their young fledglings are a revitalized, fiercely independent breed. Cursed by a spiritual conflagration in their veins, a Ravnos cannot linger in one place: if they sleep within a mile of where they slumbered within the last week, the sun's fire erupts from their own flesh. Constantly in motion, they make their homes in converted vans, train cars, and touring buses, relying on their mastery of animal scouts, stealth, and shimmering illusions to outmaneuver pursuers and extract wealth from unsuspecting marks.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

Rootless and fiercely autonomous, the Ravnos generally avoid joining the rigid Camarilla, which demands stationary havens and bureaucratic surveillance. They flourish naturally on the fringes of the Anarch Movement, offering unique services as cross-country couriers, smugglers, and saboteurs who can infiltrate a domain, sow chaos, and vanish into the night before the local Sheriff even realizes they were there.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

Ravnos look for individuals who thrive on uncertainty, quick thinking, and constant mobility. They Embrace long-distance hitchhikers, carnival performers, confidence artists, daring investigative photojournalists, motorcycle couriers, stage magicians, and venture capitalists who gamble everything on a single high-risk throw of the dice.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Midnight Courier**: A high-speed motorcycle runner who delivers confidential occult packages between domains along desolate desert highways.
- **The Illusionist Grifter**: A silver-tongued charlatan who uses Obfuscate and Presence to weave convincing phantasms, scamming corrupt politicians out of their wealth.
- **The Traveling Antiquarian**: A wandering collector who scours estate sales, pawn shops, and ancient ruins for forgotten Kindred artifacts before moving on.
- **The Infiltration Operative**: A freelance espionage specialist who enters hostile territories under assumed identities to destabilize local power structures.
