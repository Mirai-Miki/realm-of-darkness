# Clan Lasombra

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Manipulators, The Clan of Night, Magisters, Keepers, Shadows

---

## Overview

The Lasombra believe that unlife is an uncompromising social Darwinist crucible where only the ruthless, cunning, and victorious have the right to endure. Viewing humility and weakness as moral failings, the Magisters operate as consummate shadow-puppeteers, pulling the strings of religious hierarchies, political regimes, and organized crime syndicates. They do not merely seek power; they seek total, undeniable mastery, preferring the enduring influence of the power behind the throne over the vulnerable spotlight of figurehead leadership.

Channeling the cold horrors of the Abyss through the discipline of Oblivion, the Keepers can command living shadows, smothering light and choking opponents with tentacled darkness. However, their intimate affinity with darkness permanently disfigures their reflection. Mirrors, glass, and digital cameras warp and distort their likeness, while modern electronics malfunction in their presence—a devastating handicap in the 21st century that forces the clan to rely heavily on human retainers and ghouls to manage their communications.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

Historically the dark priests and leaders of the fanatic Sabbat, the Lasombra recognized the looming failure of the Gehenna Crusade and executed a calculated geopolitical defection. Through ruthless bargaining, sacrifice of elders, and strategic assassinations, the mainstream clan petitioned for entry into the Camarilla. Now integrated into the Ivory Tower, they aggressively compete against the Ventrue for administrative dominance, while rogue factions remain with the Sabbat or plot in secret as Anarch warlords.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

The Lasombra Embrace is notorious for being a brutal, orchestrated ordeal designed to break the mortal subject and test if they have the mettle to fight their way back from total ruin. Preferred candidates include ruthless corporate executives, maritime ship captains, high-ranking clergy, criminal kingpins, military strategists, and survivors of extreme personal adversity who refused to break under torture or catastrophe.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Behind-the-Throne Advisor**: A courtly manipulator who positions themselves as the indispensable seneschal or confidant to a naive young Prince.
- **The Corrupt Preacher**: A charismatic religious authority who uses fear, sin, and spiritual blackmail to control an devoted congregation of mortal worshipers.
- **The Corporate Raider**: An aggressive hedge-fund manager who strips assets, bankrupts rivals, and controls municipal real estate from soundproofed boardrooms.
- **The Shadow Operative**: An infiltration specialist who uses shadow-stepping and Dominate to bypass security systems and liquidate high-value targets.
