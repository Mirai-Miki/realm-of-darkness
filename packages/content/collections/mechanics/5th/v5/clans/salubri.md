# Clan Salubri

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Fugitives, Cyclops, Soul-Thieves, Dajjals, Saulot's Progeny

---

## Overview

The Salubri are the tragic, hunted remnants of what was once one of the most revered and noble lineages in the history of the Damned. Centuries ago, their founder Saulot was celebrated as a peaceful mystic, warrior, and healer who was said to be the first vampire to achieve Golconda—a mythical state of spiritual enlightenment that calms the Beast. However, the rise of Clan Tremere in the Middle Ages saw Saulot diablerized and the Salubri systematically hunted, slandered as soul-stealing demons, and driven to near-extinction.

Today, the Salubri are extraordinarily rare, whispered by legends to number only seven at any given time (though modern nights see small clutches emerge in hidden corners). Bearing a mystical third eye in the center of their foreheads that weeps vitae whenever they invoke their powers, they are cursed with an irresistible Blood: any vampire who tastes Salubri vitae experiences an overwhelming, intoxicating hunger that compels them to drain the Cyclops to the point of diablerie. Forced to live under deep disguise, they offer healing and spiritual solace only to those they trust with their unlives.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

The Salubri hold no official standing in any sect. In Camarilla domains, ancient propaganda still paints them as treacherous diablerists, and unscrupulous Princes often hunt them to consume their potent blood. Compassionate Anarch Barons occasionally offer sanctuary to a fugitive healer, but even among rebels, the temptation of their sweet vitae remains a constant mortal peril.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

Embraces among the Salubri are solemn, exceedingly rare, and laden with immense personal consequence. Sires choose mortals burdened by profound compassion, unshakeable moral strength, or a desperate desire to heal the broken world: trauma surgeons, hospice caregivers, selfless humanitarian aid workers, human rights defenders, and terminally ill individuals possessing transcendent spiritual grace.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Unwilling Healer**: A former emergency room physician who uses Fortitude and Auspex to mend horrific injuries in secret street clinics, constantly terrified of discovery.
- **The Seeker of Truth**: A contemplative scholar traveling under thick hoods and bandanas, researching ancient fragments of Saulot's journey toward Golconda.
- **The Disguised Bodyguard**: A silent, stoic defender who offers protective Fortitude to young fledglings while concealing their third eye beneath tactical headwear.
- **The Vengeful Monster-Hunter**: A disillusioned survivor who uses Dominate and martial discipline to eliminate depraved wights and predatory Kindred who terrorize the innocent.
