# Clan Banu Haqim

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Judges, Clan of the Hunt, Children of Haqim, Mediators

---

## Overview

The Banu Haqim perceive themselves not merely as predators of the night, but as the appointed guardians and arbiters of Kindred morality. Driven by a rigorous ancestral tradition, the Children of Haqim hold that vampiric existence is a grave responsibility rather than a license for debauchery. Historically known as the Assassins or Viziers of Alamut, modern Banu Haqim balance a deep philosophical reverence for law, honor, and discipline with a lethal capacity to execute judgment against those who transgress against moral or political order.

Central to the clan's mindset is an unrelenting pursuit of righteousness in a world defined by damnation. They reject the blind hedonism common to many young vampires, instead dedicating their unlives to codes of conduct, jurisprudence, and scholarship. In the modern era, having formally allied with the Camarilla (and maintaining longstanding ties with the Islamic Ashirra sect), they frequently serve as magistrates, enforcers, and forensic investigators. Yet, an undercurrent of tension remains: many elder vampires view the Judges' sanctimonious moralizing with suspicion, fearing both their supernatural blade-work and their terrifying thirst for Kindred vitae.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

The recent destruction of Alamut's mystical isolation and the onset of the Gehenna War forced a historic realignment. Under the leadership of the scholar Ur-Shulgi and subsequent schisms, a major faction joined the Ivory Tower, replacing the fractured Tremere as respected mystical and legal authorities. However, a spirited minority operates within the Anarch Movement, interpreting Haqim's mandate as a duty to strike down tyrannical Princes and corrupt elders rather than uphold static hierarchies.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

Banu Haqim sires seek mortals who exhibit an unshakeable sense of purpose, judicial acumen, or uncompromising devotion to a code. Favored candidates include judges, civil rights litigators, investigative detectives, philosophers, forensic scientists, and disciplined military operators who demonstrate the restraint necessary to master their predatory instincts.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Executioner**: A sanctioned investigator and lethal instrument of domain law who pursues Masquerade breachers and oathbreakers without mercy.
- **The Union Arbiter**: An Anarch-aligned mediator who organizes mortal workers and disenfranchised neonates against exploitative elders using legal and physical leverage.
- **The Esoteric Architect**: An occult scholar fascinated by the geometry of blood, building secret fortified havens and researching the metaphysical boundaries of vitae.
- **The Incorruptible Detective**: A mortal investigator brought into the night to untangle intricate Kindred conspiracies and expose hypocrisy at court.
