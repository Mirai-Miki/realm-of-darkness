# Clan Hecata

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Necromancers, Clan of Death, Lazarenes, Devil-Kindred

---

## Overview

The Hecata stand unique among Kindred as an unholy conglomerate of death-worshiping bloodlines, unified under a singular, morbid obsession: the mastery of mortality, the grave, and the underworld. While other clans trace a clean patriarchal lineage, the modern Hecata are the product of the 'Family Reunion'—a landmark necromantic pact that bound together the merchant Giovanni, remnants of ancient Cappadocians, the rotting Samedi, spectral Harbingers, blood-drinking Nagaraja, and vengeful Lamiae under the mantle of the Clan of Death.

Operating like an international syndicate of funeral moguls, occult genealogists, and predatory venture capitalists, the Hecata view death not as an ending, but as an inexhaustible commercial resource and metaphysical frontier. Through their command of Oblivion, they interrogate ghosts, bind restless wraiths into spectral espionage rings, and rot flesh with a casual touch. Yet their kiss lacks all ecstasy: a Hecata bite inflicts pure, excruciating agony, making peaceful feeding an intricate challenge of torture, restraint, or surgical bribery.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

Maintaining strict neutrality in the broader Jyhad, the Hecata refuse fealty to both Camarilla and Anarch sects. They sell their necromantic services and illicit financial infrastructure to whichever party pays the highest price, functioning as indispensable morticians and banking proxies in major cities, while jealously guarding their internal family secrets from inquisitive outsiders.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

True to their dynastic origins, many Hecata are selected directly from mortal cadet branches of the Giovanni, Dunsirn, Milliner, or Pisanob families. Outside bloodlines are recruited for specific utilitarian aptitudes: forensic pathologists, funeral home operators, estate lawyers, hospice workers, ghost hunters, and cold-blooded financiers comfortable profiting from mortality.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Forensic Specialist**: A medical examiner who tampers with mortal autopsy reports, uses Auspex to read crime scenes, and speaks to murder victims on the slab.
- **The Unscrupulous Banker**: A dynastic financier who launders ill-gotten boons and assets through off-shore shell companies and manages torpid Kindred trusts.
- **The Spectral Inquisitor**: A dedicated necromancer who commands a legion of enslaved wraiths to spy on rival Elysiums and haunt political enemies.
- **The Re-Animator**: An obsessive student of the flesh who conducts macabre experiments on corpses, crafting undead servitors for high-bidding clients.
