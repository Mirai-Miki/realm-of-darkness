# Clan Tzimisce

**Sobriquets & Monikers:** The Dragons, Voivodes, The Old Clan, Stokers, Fiends

---

## Overview

To the Tzimisce, unlife is defined by a single, unyielding concept: possession. Known in antiquity as the tyrannical voivodes of Eastern Europe and the Carpathian Mountains, the Dragons hoard people, territory, organizations, and even physical forms with the obsessive jealousy of a mythic serpent slumbering on gold. Whether commanding a feudal mountain estate or an urban tenement building, a Tzimisce views everything inside their domain as personal property to be managed, disciplined, and reshaped according to their inscrutable aesthetic will.

This possessive drive extends directly into biology through Vicissitude—the legendary art of fleshcrafting, expressed in modern nights through the Protean discipline. Tzimisce can mold bone, muscle, and flesh like warm clay, sculpting themselves into transcendent idols or horrifying war beasts, while twisting their servitors into deformed, loyal monstrous guards (szlachta). Yet their unyielding attachment to their domain is their undoing: a Dragon must sleep surrounded by their chosen charge—traditionally soil from their ancestral homeland, or specific significant objects and followers—or suffer agonizing psychological decay.

## Modern Nights & Sect Standing

Formerly the spiritual core of the Sabbat alongside the Lasombra, many Tzimisce abandoned the fanatical Gehenna War when ancient elders arose to treat their childe as pawns. Today, Dragons are widely distributed across the Anarch Movement and independent fringes, carving out private fiefdoms in city slums, biker gangs, or secluded rural estates, fiercely repelling all trespassers.

## Embraces & Mortal Stock

The Dragons Embrace those who possess an unyielding sense of territory, authority, or obsessive craftsmanship. Favored mortal stock includes feudal landholders, slum landlords, cartel bosses, plastic surgeons, sculptors, eccentric collectors, and military officers who understand the sacredness of hospitality and the iron rules of ownership.

## Notable Archetypes

- **The Slum Landlord**: A predatory real-estate owner who treats an entire high-rise apartment block and its terrified tenants as their personal living canvas.
- **The Fleshcrafting Artisan**: An eccentric body-modification artist who reshapes the physical anatomy of willing clientele into living sculptural masterpieces.
- **The Border Warlord**: A territorial biker boss who commands highway checkpoints and rustic compounds with canine packs and strict codes of hospitality.
- **The Grudge-Bearing Voivode**: An ancient traditionalist dwelling in a crumbling ancestral manor, plotting slow, generational vengeance against the Tremere who stole their lands.
