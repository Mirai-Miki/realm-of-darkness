# Fractured Perspective

**Clan:** Malkavian

## Description

The Malkavian lineage is defined by minds fundamentally altered by the Embrace, leaving every member with a permanent mental derangement. This internal chaos may manifest as vivid delusions, unsettlingly clear visions, or other unique afflictions that distance the vampire from shared reality. While this perspective can sometimes offer glimpses of hidden truths, it primarily makes the vampire's behavior unpredictable and their logic difficult for others to grasp.

## Mechanics

This curse comes to the forefront whenever the Malkavian suffers a **Bestial Failure** or a **Compulsion**. For the duration of the scene, the character suffers a penalty equal to their **Bane Severity** to all dice pools within a specific category: **Physical, Social, or Mental**.

- This penalty is applied in addition to any existing penalties from Compulsions.
- The exact nature of the affliction and the dice category it affects are determined between the player and the Storyteller during character creation.
