# Violent Temper

**Clan:** Brujah

## Description

The Brujah are defined by a volatile lineage, often earning them the moniker "The Rabble." Their vitae carries a supernatural irritability that makes them prone to sudden, explosive outbursts. Unlike other Kindred who might struggle with their inner Beast, the Brujah find their rage is a constant, simmering presence that is easily triggered by even minor provocations.

## Mechanics

When a Brujah is forced to make a roll to resist a **Fury Frenzy**, they suffer a penalty to their dice pool. The player must subtract a number of dice equal to the character's **Bane Severity** from the total. Regardless of the penalty, the dice pool cannot be reduced below a minimum of one die.
