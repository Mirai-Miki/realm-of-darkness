# Blood Bond Deficiency

**Clan:** Tremere

## Description

Historically, the Tremere were defined by the Pyramid—a strict, top-down hierarchy maintained through mandatory Blood Bonds. Following the fall of Vienna, their vitae underwent a fundamental change, recoiling from these mystical connections. Consequently, Tremere blood has lost its ability to ensnare other Kindred, though the Tremere themselves remain vulnerable to the bonds of other clans. While they can still bind mortals and ghouls, their tainted vitae requires more effort to establish even those lesser links.

## Mechanics

Tremere vitae is incapable of forming a Blood Bond with other Kindred. However, Tremere vampires can still be Blood Bound by Kindred of other clans as normal.

- They are still able to bind mortals and ghouls, but the process is more demanding.
- A mortal or ghoul must drink the Tremere's vitae an additional number of times equal to the vampire’s **Bane Severity** before the bond is successfully established.
