# Noxious Blood

**Clan:** Banu Haqim

## Description

For some members of the clan, the Blood carries a lethal toxicity that is harmless to other vampires but deadly to mortals. This makes the standard practices of the Kindred—such as creating ghouls or establishing Blood Bonds with humans—extremely risky. The vitae of these vampires acts as a poison rather than a gift, often killing the very mortals the Banu Haqim seek to empower or enslave.

## Mechanics

Any mortal who ingests the blood of a Banu Haqim with this bane suffers **Aggravated Damage** equal to the vampire's **Bane Severity** for every Rouse Check's worth of vitae consumed.

- This blood cannot be used to heal mortal injuries.
- The toxic property only inflicts damage when ingested in quantities sufficient to establish a Blood Bond; trace amounts do not trigger this effect.
