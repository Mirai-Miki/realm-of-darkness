# Aversio

**Clan:** Ministry

## Description

The vitae of the Ministry reacts with intense hostility to illumination. This supernatural aversion causes members of the clan to recoil instinctively when exposed to direct light, whether its source is natural or artificial. They are truly creatures of the shadows, finding that bright environments actively suppress their ability to focus and utilize their vampiric power.

## Mechanics

While subjected to bright light directed straight at them, a Minister suffers a penalty to all dice pools equal to their **Bane Severity**. Additionally, the vampire is more vulnerable to the sun's touch; add their **Bane Severity** to any **Aggravated damage** sustained from exposure to sunlight.
