# Cursed Courtesy

**Clan:** Tzimisce

## Description

Just as the Tzimisce are obsessively protective of their own property, they are supernaturally compelled to respect the boundaries of others. Entering a private residence without a formal invitation causes the vampire intense psychological distress and a visceral urge to flee. This curse makes clandestine home invasions or uninvited visits to other Kindred havens an exhausting and physically draining ordeal for the Dragon.

## Mechanics

A Tzimisce vampire who wishes to enter a place of residence (including both mortal homes and Kindred havens) without an invitation must endure significant penalties:

- The vampire must spend a number of **Willpower** points equal to their **Bane Severity** to enter.
- While inside the uninvited location, the vampire suffers a penalty to all **Discipline dice pools** equal to their **Bane Severity**.
- The invitation must be extended by a legitimate resident of the location.
- Public spaces or abandoned buildings do not trigger this effect, though individual apartments or flats within a larger building are considered separate, private homes.
- A character with this bane is prohibited from taking the **Folkloric Block (Invitation Only)** Flaw.
