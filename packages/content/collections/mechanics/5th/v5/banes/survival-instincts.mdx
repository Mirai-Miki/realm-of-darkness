# Survival Instincts

**Clan:** Gangrel

## Description

For many Gangrel, survival is the only priority that matters. Their instincts are finely tuned to ensure they endure even the most hostile environments and threats. However, this hyper-focus on self-preservation makes the Beast within them particularly reactive to things that threaten their unlife, such as fire or sunlight.

## Mechanics

When a Gangrel with this bane is forced to make a roll to resist **Terror Frenzy** (fear frenzy), they suffer a penalty to their dice pool. Subtract a number of dice equal to the vampire's **Bane Severity** from the total. This penalty cannot reduce the final dice pool below a minimum of one die.
