# Repulsiveness

**Clan:** Nosferatu

## Description

The Nosferatu are defined by a physical corruption so profound that they can never truly pass for human in their natural state. This hideousness is not merely skin-deep; it is a fundamental part of their vampiric nature that warps their flesh into something monstrous. Even when using supernatural means to hide their appearance, the inherent wrongness of their Blood makes the deception difficult to maintain.

## Mechanics

All Nosferatu with this bane automatically possess the **Repulsive Flaw (-2)** and are prohibited from ever increasing their rating in the **Looks Merit**.

- Any attempt to disguise themselves as human—whether through mundane means or the use of Obfuscate powers like **Mask of a Thousand Faces** and **Impostor’s Guise**—suffers a dice pool penalty equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
