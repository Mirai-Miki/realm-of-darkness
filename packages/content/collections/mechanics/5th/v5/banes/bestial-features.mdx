# Bestial Features

**Clan:** Gangrel

## Description

The Gangrel maintain a wary partnership with their inner Beast, but this connection often leaves a mark. When a Gangrel loses control and enters a frenzy, they manifest physical or behavioral traits of an animal. These "bestial features" act as a lingering reminder of their feral nature, persisting long after the initial outburst has ended.

## Mechanics

During a frenzy, a Gangrel manifests a number of animalistic features equal to their **Bane Severity**. These can be physical changes (like fur or slit pupils), scents (like a predatory musk), or specific behavioral tics.

- Each feature reduces one **Attribute** by 1 point.
- The specific Attribute penalized depends on the feature (e.g., bat-like ears might reduce Resolve due to sensory distraction, while a bear-like musk might reduce Charisma).
- If no specific Attribute seems appropriate, the penalty is applied to **Intelligence** or **Manipulation**.
- These features and their associated penalties last for the remainder of the night of the frenzy and one full night afterward.
- If the character **Rides the Wave** of their frenzy, they only manifest a single feature and suffer only one Attribute penalty.
