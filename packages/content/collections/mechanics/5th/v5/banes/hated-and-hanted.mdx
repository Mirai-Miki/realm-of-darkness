# Hated and Hunted

**Clan:** Salubri

## Description

The Salubri are the remnants of a nearly extinct lineage, pursued by other Kindred who find their vitae supernaturally addictive. Beyond this "hunted" status, every member of the clan bears a physical mark of their heritage: a third eye centered on the forehead. This eye is a permanent fixture that cannot be masked by mystical means, and it reacts visibly whenever the vampire draws upon their power, making it a constant threat to their anonymity.

## Mechanics

- **Addictive Blood:** Any non-Salubri vampire who consumes enough Salubri blood to slake at least one level of Hunger must immediately test for **Hunger Frenzy**. The Difficulty is **2 + the Salubri's Bane Severity**. For members of the **Banu Haqim** clan, the Difficulty increases to **3 + the Salubri's Bane Severity**. If the test is failed, the vampire continues to feed uncontrollably until they are physically forced away.
- **The Third Eye:** All Salubri possess a third eye on their forehead. This eye cannot be hidden by any supernatural power, though it can be obscured by physical items like hoods or bandages.
- **Weeping Vitae:** Whenever a Salubri activates a **Discipline power**, the third eye weeps vitae. The intensity of the blood flow matches the level of the power being used. This discharge is significant enough to trigger a **Hunger Frenzy test** for any nearby vampires who have a **Hunger of 4 or higher**.
