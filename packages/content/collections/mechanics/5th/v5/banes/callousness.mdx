# Callousness

**Clan:** Lasombra

## Description

Whether due to their tradition of Embracing the most ruthless individuals or an inherent psychic rot, the Lasombra are prone to a rapid slide into inhumanity. There is an unsettling, "off" quality to their presence that suggests a lack of human empathy. For these vampires, maintaining a connection to their former mortality is a constant struggle, as their inner Beast is always ready to further erode their remaining Humanity.

## Mechanics

- When a Lasombra character makes a **Remorse roll**, they suffer a penalty to their dice pool.
- Deduct a number of dice equal to the character's **Bane Severity** from the roll.
- This penalty cannot reduce the dice pool below a minimum of one die.
