# Unnatural Manifestations

**Clan:** Malkavian

## Description

Even those Malkavians who appear mundane carry an eerie quality that becomes apparent when they tap into their Blood. This manifesting weirdness causes an instinctive unease in those nearby. While mortals may not understand why they feel disturbed, they react with sudden fear or dislike toward the vampire. To other Kindred, this sensation serves as an immediate identifier of the Malkavian's supernatural nature.

## Mechanics

Whenever a Malkavian activates any **Discipline power**, mortals in close proximity (roughly within the same room) become spooked for the remainder of the scene.

- The vampire suffers a dice penalty equal to their **Bane Severity** on all social interaction rolls with these mortals, except for **Intimidation** tests.
- While this effect is not an automatic Masquerade breach, the affected mortals will feel a sudden, lasting aversion to the vampire.
- Other vampires in the vicinity experience a similar sensation and will instantly recognize the Malkavian as a vampire, though they do not suffer any dice penalties to their interactions.
