# Hierarchy

**Clan:** Ventrue

## Description

While the Ventrue are often the ones giving orders, they are also supernaturally predisposed to following them—especially when they come from those with older Blood. This instinctive deference to seniority and lineage is a core part of their nature. Whether it manifests as an adherence to ancient feudal codes or a more modern corporate pragmatism, a Ventrue finds it difficult to assert their will against those who sit higher on the generational ladder.

## Mechanics

This bane imposes limitations on how a Ventrue interacts with vampires of a lower generation.

- The vampire suffers a penalty to their **Discipline dice pools** equal to their **Bane Severity** when attempting to use their supernatural powers on any vampire of a lower generation.
- If the Ventrue wishes to launch a direct physical or social attack against a vampire of a lower generation, they must first spend a number of **Willpower** points equal to their **Bane Severity**.
