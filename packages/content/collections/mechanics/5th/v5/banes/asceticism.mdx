# Asceticism

**Clan:** Salubri

## Description

Some Salubri find that their Blood is only truly responsive when they are in a state of starvation. These individuals possess a nature that resists the vampiric condition; the more they satisfy their Hunger, the more their supernatural abilities begin to fail them. To wield their full power, they must walk a dangerous line, maintaining a state of constant craving to ensure their Disciplines remain effective.

## Mechanics

- Whenever the vampire's **Hunger** level is below 3, they suffer a penalty equal to their **Bane Severity** to all **Discipline dice pools**.
- This penalty is in addition to the complications caused by the third eye, such as the weeping of vitae when powers are activated.
