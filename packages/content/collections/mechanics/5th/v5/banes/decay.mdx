# Decay

**Clan:** Hecata

## Description

Some Hecata are so deeply saturated with the energies of the grave that they radiate a palpable aura of entropy. This rot affects everything in their immediate vicinity; plants wither, inorganic materials become brittle and decrepit, and living servants often develop a sickly, haggard appearance. This constant decay makes maintaining a high-quality lifestyle or a well-kept haven an uphill battle, as the vampire's very presence slowly erodes their assets and social connections.

## Mechanics

Hecata with this bane suffer from an inherent rot that affects their social and physical standing.

- The character must take additional dots in **Flaws** equal to their **Bane Severity**. These dots must be distributed among **Retainer**, **Haven**, or **Resources** flaws.
- These flaws can be "bought off" by paying double the normal amount of Background dots.
- Furthermore, purchasing new dots in the **Retainer**, **Haven**, or **Resources** Advantages costs an additional amount of experience points equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
