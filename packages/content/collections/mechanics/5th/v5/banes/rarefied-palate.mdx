# Rarefied Palate

**Clan:** Ventrue

## Description

The Ventrue are known for their extremely selective tastes, possessing what is often described as a "rarefied palate." A Blue Blood cannot simply nourish themselves on any mortal they encounter; their Blood demands a very specific type of vessel. These preferences are highly individual and can range from physical traits like hair color or heritage to more specific criteria like a person's profession, life experiences, or even the presence of certain substances in their system. Attempting to consume blood that does not meet these exacting standards is a repulsive experience for the vampire, often resulting in their body violently rejecting the meal.

## Mechanics

A Ventrue character must select a specific type of prey as their feeding preference.

- If the vampire attempts to feed from a mortal who does not fit this preference, they must spend a number of **Willpower** points equal to their **Bane Severity**.
- Failure to spend this Willpower results in the vampire immediately vomiting the blood back up.
- A Ventrue can attempt to sense if a mortal meets their specific requirements by making a **Resolve + Awareness** test (Difficulty 4 or higher).
