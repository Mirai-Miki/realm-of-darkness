# Cold-Blooded

**Clan:** Ministry

## Description

The blood of some Ministers is exceptionally cold, mirroring the nature of the serpents they are often associated with. Without the warmth of fresh vitae circulating through them, these vampires find it nearly impossible to maintain the illusion of life. Mimicking a heartbeat or a breath is not an intuitive act for them, but a strenuous exertion that requires recent feeding to even attempt.

## Mechanics

A Ministry vampire with this bane can only utilize the **Blush of Life** if they have fed from a living vessel within the same scene or roughly the last hour (at the Storyteller's discretion).

- Activating the Blush of Life is more taxing for these vampires, requiring a number of **Rouse Checks** equal to their **Bane Severity** instead of the standard single check.
