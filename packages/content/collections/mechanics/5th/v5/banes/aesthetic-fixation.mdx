# Aesthetic Fixation

**Clan:** Toreador

## Description

The Toreador are fundamentally driven by an intense craving for beauty and artistic perfection. This obsession is so deeply rooted in their Blood that they find themselves physically and mentally affected by their environment. While they thrive in the presence of excellence, they suffer significantly when forced to endure surroundings they perceive as drab, poorly designed, or aesthetically repulsive. This fixation also leaves them vulnerable to falling into deep, rapt trances when confronted with something truly beautiful.

## Mechanics

While a Toreador is in an environment they consider less than beautiful, they suffer a penalty to their effectiveness.

- Subtract a number of dice equal to the vampire's **Bane Severity** from any dice pools used to activate or utilize **Disciplines**.
- The Storyteller determines the specific degree of beauty or ugliness of the surroundings (including elements like clothing or the appearance of vessels) based on the character's personal aesthetic standards.
- In addition to environmental penalties, a **Bestial Failure** often results in the vampire falling into a rapt trance, as detailed in the rules for Toreador Compulsions.
