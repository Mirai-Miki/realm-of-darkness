# Stolen Blood

**Clan:** Tremere

## Description

Because the Tremere originally usurped the vampiric curse through sorcery rather than receiving it naturally, their connection to the Blood is often less efficient than that of other lineages. While they remain masters of channelling vitae into complex sorcery, they find it much harder to use that same blood to force their bodies into performing superhuman feats. For these vampires, pushing their physical limits is a taxing ordeal that rapidly drains their internal reserves.

## Mechanics

Whenever a Tremere vampire performs a **Blood Surge**, the effort is significantly more draining.

- The player must make a number of **Rouse Checks** equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
- If these Rouse Checks would increase the vampire’s Hunger to 5 or higher, the player must choose between two options: they may either cancel the Blood Surge entirely to avoid the risk, or they may complete the surge and immediately set their Hunger level to 5.
