# Violence

**Clan:** Brujah

## Description

For some Brujah, the curse of their Blood manifests not as a loss of temper, but as an inescapable tie to destruction. As eternal rebels, they often find that their attempts to change the world—or even simple interactions—result in unintended harm. This variant is often used in stories where traditional frenzy rules are less frequent, focusing instead on the collateral damage caused by the vampire's predatory nature.

## Mechanics

Whenever a Brujah achieves a **Messy Critical** on any Skill test, they automatically inflict damage on the target of that action. The damage dealt is equal to the vampire's **Bane Severity** and is added to the standard results of the Hunger dice.

- The storyteller determines if the damage is physical or mental based on the situation.
- This damage is **Aggravated** by default. However, the player may spend one point of **Willpower** to make the damage **Superficial** instead.
