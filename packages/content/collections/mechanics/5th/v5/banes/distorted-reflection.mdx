# Distorted Reflection

**Clan:** Lasombra

## Description

The Lasombra are partially untethered from the physical world, resulting in a supernatural distortion of their image and voice. They do not appear clearly in mirrors or on digital recordings; instead, their presence manifests as a flickering, warped, or translucent anomaly. While an average observer might dismiss this as a technical glitch or an irregularity in a surface, those familiar with the Kindred immediately recognize the hallmark of the Clan of Shadows. This distortion extends to audio, making even basic communication through modern devices a significant challenge.

## Mechanics

- Anyone viewing a reflection or recording (live or otherwise) of a Lasombra can identify their nature if they know what to look for. Uninformed observers will notice a distortion but will likely attribute it to technical errors or surface irregularities.
- This effect does not hide the vampire's identity with certainty, nor does it make them less likely to be captured by surveillance.
- Utilizing modern communication technology, such as making a phone call, requires a **Technology test** with a Difficulty of **2 + Bane Severity**.
- Any attempts to avoid electronic vampire detection systems suffer a penalty equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
