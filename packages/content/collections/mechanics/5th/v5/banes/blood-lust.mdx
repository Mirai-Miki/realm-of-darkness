# Blood Lust

**Clan:** Banu Haqim

## Description

The Banu Haqim are often called the Judges of the Kindred, and their vitae carries an inherent drive to punish those they perceive as transgressors. This impulse is most dangerous when directed toward other vampires. When one of these Kindred tastes the blood of another Cainite, they are struck by an overwhelming addiction that is incredibly difficult to resist. This thirst can lead to a loss of control that endangers both the vampire and their victim.

## Mechanics

Whenever a Banu Haqim slakes at least one level of Hunger by drinking the blood of another vampire, they must immediately perform a test to resist a **Hunger Frenzy**.

- The Difficulty of this test is equal to **2 + the character's Bane Severity**.
- If the test is failed, the vampire enters a frenzy and attempts to gorge themselves on the victim's vitae, potentially leading to the act of diablerie.
