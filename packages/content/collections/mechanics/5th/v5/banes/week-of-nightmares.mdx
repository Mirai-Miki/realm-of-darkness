# Week of Nightmares

**Clan:** Ravnos

## Description

The Ravnos carry a terrible legacy within their vitae. The same solar fire that consumed their progenitor continues to burn within the blood of the lineage, threatening to erupt from their flesh if they ever remain stationary for too long. This curse forces the Ravnos into a nomadic existence, as the very earth seems to reject their presence if they attempt to establish a permanent home.

## Mechanics

If a Ravnos slumbers in the same location more than once in a seven-night period, the fire in their blood begins to scorch them from within.

- Every time the vampire spends the day in a location they have already occupied within the last week, the player must roll a number of dice equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
- The vampire suffers **Aggravated damage** equal to the number of 10s (critical results) rolled.
- This effect also applies to Ravnos currently in torpor.
- Unless the Storyteller defines the scope differently for the chronicle, a "location" is considered any area within a one-mile radius.
- Mobile havens (such as vans or trailers) are safe as long as they are parked at least one mile away from the previous night's resting spot.
- Ravnos characters are prohibited from taking the **No Haven** Flaw during character creation.
