# Painful Kiss

**Clan:** Hecata

## Description

While the bite of most Kindred induces a state of supernatural ecstasy, the Hecata are cursed with fangs that bring only searing agony. The "Kiss" of a Lazarene is a traumatic experience of pure torture, lacking any of the sedative effects found in other lineages. This makes feeding a violent and messy affair, as victims will instinctively and desperately struggle to escape the intense pain of the vampire's embrace.

## Mechanics

When a Hecata vampire drinks directly from a victim, the process is always agonizing and mechanically considered a harmful drink, resulting in blood loss.

- **Unwilling Mortals:** If not physically restrained, they will immediately attempt to flee or fight back.
- **Coerced or Willing Mortals:** Even those who intend to submit must succeed in a **Stamina + Resolve** test (Difficulty 2 + the character's **Bane Severity**) to avoid recoiling or pulling away from the pain.
- **Vampire Victims:** Any vampire fed upon by a Hecata must succeed in a **Frenzy test** (Difficulty 3) to avoid falling into a **Terror Frenzy**.
