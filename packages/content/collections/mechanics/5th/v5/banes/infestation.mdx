# Infestation

**Clan:** Nosferatu

## Description

Rather than physical deformity, some Nosferatu are cursed to be followed by the lowest forms of animal life. Swarms of rats, roaches, or pigeons are drawn to the vampire like a beacon, leaving filth and chaos in their wake. This constant presence makes it nearly impossible for the vampire to maintain a peaceful environment or a clean haven, as the vermin inevitably drive others away and make concentration difficult.

## Mechanics

A Nosferatu with this bane finds that any **Haven** they occupy is permanently infested with vermin.

- This infestation imposes a penalty equal to **2 + the character's Bane Severity** to any activity requiring concentration or to social tests (at the Storyteller's discretion) performed within the haven.
- If the vampire spends a single scene in any enclosed location, that area becomes temporarily infested, causing a penalty equal to the character's **Bane Severity** to similar rolls.
- Any use of **Animalism** to temporarily control the vermin brought about by this bane is performed at a penalty equal to the character's **Bane Severity**.
- Unlike the standard Nosferatu bane, vampires with this variant are not necessarily deformed, though they may still possess an unsettling or strange appearance.
