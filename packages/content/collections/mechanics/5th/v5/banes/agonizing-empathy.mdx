# Agonizing Empathy

**Clan:** Toreador

## Description

Some Divas experience their curse as an overwhelming, literal sensory connection to those they interact with. This manifests most painfully during the act of feeding. Rather than remaining detached, the vampire physically experiences the trauma they inflict on their vessel. This supernatural empathy causes the vampire's own body to mimic the injuries sustained by the mortal, turning the pleasure of the Kiss into a shared experience of agony.

## Mechanics

Whenever a Toreador with this bane inflicts damage on a mortal while feeding, they suffer a reflexive physical backlash.

- The vampire suffers the same amount of damage as the mortal (typically **Aggravated** damage).
- This reflected damage is capped at a maximum value equal to the character's **Bane Severity** per feeding session.
- The injury manifests as involuntary internal bleeding and visible bruising on the vampire's body, appearing in the exact location where they bit their victim.
