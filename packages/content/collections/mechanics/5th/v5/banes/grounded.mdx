# Grounded

**Clan:** Tzimisce

## Description

The Tzimisce are supernaturally tethered to a specific "charge" that defines their identity and sense of ownership. This charge is a clearly defined and limited focus, such as a specific plot of land, a particular group of people, or a specific organization. To maintain their spiritual and mental stability, a member of this clan must spend their daysleep in close proximity to this chosen charge. While traditional Tzimisce often sleep in the soil of their ancestral lands, modern Dragons might surround themselves with members of a subculture they lead or sleep within a specific building they have claimed as their own.

## Mechanics

A Tzimisce must choose a specific, limited charge (a domain, group, or organization). They must spend their daysleep surrounded by this charge.

- If the vampire slumbers away from their charge, they suffer **Aggravated Willpower damage** equal to their **Bane Severity** upon waking the following night.
