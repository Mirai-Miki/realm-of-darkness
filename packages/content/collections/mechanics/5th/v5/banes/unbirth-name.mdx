# Unbirth Name

**Clan:** Ravnos

## Description

Some Ravnos anchor their identity to the name they held as a mortal—their "unbirth name." While they use this name to maintain a sense of self while navigating a world of illusions, it also represents a profound mystical weakness. The Ravnos take extreme precautions to keep this name hidden, as anyone who discovers it and speaks it aloud gains significant power over the vampire's mind and supernatural defenses.

## Mechanics

An individual who knows a Ravnos' unbirth name and speaks it directly to them gains a supernatural advantage over the vampire.

- The opponent receives a bonus equal to the Ravnos' **Bane Severity** on all rolls made to resist the vampire's **Discipline powers**.
- The Ravnos suffers a penalty equal to their **Bane Severity** on any rolls made to resist supernatural powers used against them by the person wielding their name.
